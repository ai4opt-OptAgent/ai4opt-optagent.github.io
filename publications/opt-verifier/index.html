ICML 2026 / Verification & correction

Opt-Verifier: Unleashing the Power of LLMs for Optimization Modeling via Dual-Side Verification

Haoyang Liu, Jie Wang, Boxuan Niu, Xiongwei Han, Yian Xu, Mingxuan Ye, Zijie Geng, Fangzhou Zhu, Tao Zhong, Mingxuan Yuan, Jianye Hao

Read the paper↗Cite this work
Conceptual overview · illustrative, not an experimental result

Check both the model structure and its solutions to make optimization modeling more reliable.

01 / Research question

How can we detect a modeling error when the generated program still runs?

02 / Approach

Dual-side verification compares the model structure with the problem requirements and examines whether its solutions are valid.

03 / Research scope

The paper studies automated verification and correction of LLM-generated optimization models on multiple benchmarks.
