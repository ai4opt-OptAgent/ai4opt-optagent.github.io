OptiBench Meets ReSocratic: Measure and Improve LLMs for Optimization Modeling

Zhicheng Yang, Yiwei Wang, Yinya Huang, Zhijiang Guo, Wei Shi, Xiongwei Han, Liang Feng, Linqi Song, Xiaodan Liang, Jing Tang

Read the paper↗Cite this work
Conceptual overview · illustrative, not an experimental result

Measure end-to-end modeling and synthesize learning data from mathematical formulations.

01 / Research question

How can we evaluate realistic optimization tasks and create useful modeling demonstrations?

02 / Approach

OptiBench covers linear and nonlinear problems with optional tabular data. ReSocratic synthesizes formulations before translating them into questions.

03 / Research scope

A benchmark and data synthesis study connecting optimization evaluation with model training.
