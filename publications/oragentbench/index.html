ORAgentBench: Can LLM Agents Solve Challenging Operations Research Tasks End to End?

Jiajun Li, Mingshu Cai, Yixuan Li, Yu Ding, Ran Hou, Guanyu Nie, Xiongwei Han, Wanyuan Wang

Read the paper↗Project↗Code↗Cite this work

ORAgentBench evaluates autonomous agents on end-to-end operations research workflows, from reading operational artifacts to building models, executing solvers, and submitting validated decisions.

01 / Research question

Can an autonomous agent turn a natural-language brief and multiple operational data files into a feasible, high-quality decision? ORAgentBench studies the full workflow connecting problem understanding, modeling, implementation, and solution improvement.

02 / Approach

Each task runs in an isolated environment with an operational brief, data, configuration artifacts, and a submission schema. Agents inspect files, choose modeling and solving strategies, write and run code, and submit decision artifacts. Hidden validators check the submission format, hard-constraint feasibility, and normalized objective quality.

03 / Research scope

The arXiv preprint introduces 107 human-reviewed executable tasks covering planning, routing, scheduling, inventory, and other OR scenarios. Evaluation includes fourteen model-agent configurations and reports feasibility, normalized quality, and pass rate across difficulty levels.
