DeepOR: A Deep Reasoning Foundation Model for Optimization Modeling

Ziyang Xiao, Yuan Jessica Wang, Xiongwei Han, Shisi Guan, Jingyan Zhu, Jingrong Xie, Lilin Xu, Han Wu, Wing Yin Yu, Zehua Liu, Xiaojin Fu, Gang Chen, Dongxiang Zhang

Read the paper↗Cite this work
Conceptual overview · illustrative, not an experimental result

Build domain-specific reasoning capabilities for translating decision problems into optimization models.

01 / Research question

How can a language model learn to reason through difficult optimization modeling tasks?

02 / Approach

Flowchart-guided reasoning data, supervised fine-tuning, and solver-feedback rewards develop reasoning capabilities for optimization modeling.

03 / Research scope

The study examines a specialized reasoning model on optimization modeling benchmarks.
