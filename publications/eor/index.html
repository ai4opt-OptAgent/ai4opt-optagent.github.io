ICLR 2025 / What-if & explanation

Decision Information Meets Large Language Models: The Future of Explainable Operations Research

Yansen Zhang, Qingcan Kang, Wing Yin Yu, Hailei Gong, Xiaojin Fu, Xiongwei Han, Tao Zhong, Chen Ma

Read the paper↗Cite this work
Conceptual overview · illustrative, not an experimental result

Connect what-if changes to understandable explanations of optimization decisions.

01 / Research question

How can users understand what changed in a model and why the resulting decision changed?

02 / Approach

Decision Information captures model changes arising from what-if analysis. Graph-based representations and language models support explanations.

03 / Research scope

The work studies model revision and explanation, alongside an industrial evaluation benchmark.
