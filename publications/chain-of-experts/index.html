ICLR 2024 / Modeling agents & reasoning

Chain-of-Experts: When LLMs Meet Complex Operations Research Problems

Ziyang Xiao, Dongxiang Zhang, Yangjun Wu, Lilin Xu, Yuan Wang, Xiongwei Han, Xiaojin Fu, Tao Zhong, Jia Zeng, Mingli Song, Gang Chen

Read the paper↗Cite this work
Conceptual overview · illustrative, not an experimental result

Coordinate specialized experts to model and program complex operations research problems.

01 / Research question

How can specialized language-model agents collaborate on complex operations research tasks?

02 / Approach

A conductor coordinates experts through forward thought construction and backward reflection. ComplexOR supports evaluation.

03 / Research scope

The study explores cooperative agents for automatic mathematical modeling and programming.
