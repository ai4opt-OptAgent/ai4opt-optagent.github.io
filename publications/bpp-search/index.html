ACL 2025 / Modeling agents & reasoning

BPP-Search: Enhancing Tree of Thought Reasoning for Mathematical Modeling Problem Solving

Teng Wang, Wing Yin Yu, Zhenqi He, Zehua Liu, Hailei Gong, Han Wu, Xiongwei Han, Wei Shi, Ruifeng She, Fangzhou Zhu, Tao Zhong

Read the paper↗Data↗Repository↗Cite this work
Conceptual overview · illustrative, not an experimental result

Combine beam search, process rewards, and pairwise preferences for modeling reasoning.

01 / Research question

How can a search process distinguish promising modeling steps and choose a final candidate?

02 / Approach

BPP-Search combines process rewards with beam search and a preference model. StructuredOR supplies detailed modeling annotations.

03 / Research scope

The work studies search-based modeling and releases a structured training and evaluation resource.
