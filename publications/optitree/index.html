NeurIPS 2025 / Modeling agents & reasoning

OptiTree: Hierarchical Thoughts Generation with Tree Search for LLM Optimization Modeling

Haoyang Liu, Jie Wang, Yuyang Cai, Xiongwei Han, Yufei Kuang, Jianye Hao

Read the paper↗Code↗Cite this work
Conceptual overview · illustrative, not an experimental result

Decompose complex problems through a hierarchy of reusable modeling thoughts.

01 / Research question

How can modeling decomposition adapt to a problem's mathematical structure?

02 / Approach

A modeling tree organizes problem categories and high-level thoughts. Search retrieves simpler subproblems and integrates their modeling ideas.

03 / Research scope

The method studies adaptive thought generation for complex optimization models.
